# Operational Excellence

## Automate delivery and changes

Describe infrastructure as code, deploy through a pipeline, and prefer small, frequent, reversible changes. Use progressive rollouts such as canary releases so that a bad change affects few users and can be rolled back quickly.

## Understand the state of the workload

Emit metrics, logs and traces from every component, define the indicators that show customer impact, and alert on those rather than on every internal fluctuation. Dashboards should answer whether users are being served.

## Prepare for operations

Write runbooks for known failures and playbooks for unknown ones, run game days, and hold blameless post-incident reviews. Feed what is learned back into automation and documentation.

## Evolve continuously

Review operational metrics on a schedule, retire manual steps that can be automated, and share lessons across teams.
