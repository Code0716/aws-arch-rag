# Sustainability

## Maximize utilization

Right-size resources and use auto scaling so that provisioned capacity is close to what is needed. Idle or lightly used servers consume energy without doing useful work, so consolidate workloads or move them to managed and serverless services.

## Reduce the data and compute you need

Process only the data required, compress and expire data that is no longer used, and run batch and training jobs when and where they are needed rather than continuously. Efficient code and algorithms reduce both cost and energy use.

## Choose efficient hardware and regions

Prefer newer, more efficient instance types, and consider regions with a lower carbon intensity when latency and data residency allow.

## Measure and improve

Define a sustainability metric such as resource use per unit of business output, track it over time, and include it in regular reviews.
